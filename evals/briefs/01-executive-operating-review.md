# Executive operating review

Create an 8-slide operating review for a VP audience. The organization reduced Sev-1 incidents from 19 to 11 quarter-over-quarter, but customer-impact minutes rose 14% because two incidents lasted much longer. The audience must decide whether to fund a reliability program focused on containment speed.

Requirements:
- lead with the apparent paradox;
- distinguish incident count from impact duration;
- include one native chart;
- end with a clear funding decision and expected measurable outcome;
- restrained executive visual style; no generic gradient-card deck.
