# Holdout: museum exhibition pitch

Create a 7-slide concept pitch for a museum exhibition about the physical infrastructure of the internet: undersea cables, landing stations, repair ships, and data centers.

Requirements:
- visual direction must derive from the subject rather than corporate presentation conventions;
- use archival/editorial image treatments;
- very little body copy;
- one map-like diagram should remain editable;
- no technology-neon aesthetic.
