# Startup fundraising

Create a 12-slide seed deck for a company automating compliance evidence collection for mid-market SaaS companies. Use hypothetical numbers only where clearly labeled as assumptions; do not invent customer logos or traction.

Requirements:
- strong visual opening;
- problem should be concrete, not generic fear marketing;
- business model and wedge must be understandable in under 30 seconds;
- use bespoke generated imagery only where it adds narrative value;
- no “revolutionizing compliance” language.
