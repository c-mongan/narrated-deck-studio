# Narrated Product Demo

For end-to-end production, load `../narrated-deck-studio/SKILL.md` and follow its film route. The self-contained workflow, producer brief and final-file QA helper are maintained there.

Build one plausible actor/task/outcome journey before listing features. Rehearse real search, document preview and consequential actions before narration generation. Demonstration records must be labelled; their counts and permissions do not establish live product coverage. Product changes and hosted data changes require their own task authority.

Use `../voice-clone-studio/SKILL.md` for authorised voice production and `../video-narration-sync/SKILL.md` for alignment mechanics when needed. Source permissions remain required. Reuse the accepted voice within its consent scope. Preserve the selected continuous master and make new candidates for audio revisions.

Requested caption/disclosure choices belong in the production brief; do not assume a subtitle requirement for every MP4. Deliver the requested film and useful supporting transcript/reports. Do not claim full listening, full playback, mobile playback or independent viewer acceptance unless actually performed.
