# Evidence rules

- Distinguish sourced fact, analysis, estimate, and recommendation.
- Never fabricate metrics to make a slide look complete.
- Preserve source provenance in speaker notes or a references appendix when appropriate.
- Charts should answer a specific question, not merely display available data.
- Put units, date ranges, sample sizes, and important caveats close to the evidence they qualify.
- If evidence is weak, change the claim rather than decorating it more aggressively.
