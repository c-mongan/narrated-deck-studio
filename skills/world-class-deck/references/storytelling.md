# Storytelling rules

A presentation is an argument over time, not a stack of pages.

## Slide contract

Every content slide should answer:

1. What is the claim?
2. What evidence earns the claim?
3. Why is this visual form the fastest way to understand it?
4. Why does the next slide logically follow?

## Action titles

Prefer:
- “Two failure modes account for 73% of incidents”
- “The new workflow cuts median triage from 18 to 6 minutes”

Avoid:
- “Incident Analysis”
- “Our Results”
- “Key Findings”

## Sequence

Use deliberate rhythm. A common strong pattern is:

`hook → stakes → evidence → mechanism → proof → decision → next action`

Do not force this template if another story is more natural.

## Density

Give dense evidence slides breathing room before/after. Not every slide should use the same grid, card count, or visual weight.
